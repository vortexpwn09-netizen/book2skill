# Deep Work

AI agents are systems that use context and memory. They rely on structured workflows to interpret tasks and make decisions.

A key principle is to keep actions focused on the most important objective. The design should be simple, clear, and anchored in reliable evidence.

The method is to plan first, gather context, then execute with a disciplined process. This workflow reduces noise and improves decision quality.

Another important concept is the workflow pattern. The architecture of a good system supports iteration, validation, and continuous improvement.
